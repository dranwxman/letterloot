LetterLoot

The daily word treasure hunt where every letter has a value.

One fresh board each day, the same for every player in the world. Spell words, stack bonus tiles, and haul yer loot through five levels.

How It Plays

Tap any tiles, in any order. No adjacency rules, just yer vocabulary and a sharp eye for treasure.

💎

Letters Have Value

Common letters are worth a few points. Rare ones pay big: Q, Z and J are pure gold.

✨

Bonus Tiles

Gold tiles double a letter, purple tiles triple it. Build yer words around them.

🌟

Clear the Board

Use every tile to clear a level and claim the bonus. Five levels, each bigger than the last.

🎯

Word of the Day

A fresh word to hunt down every day, with extra loot for finding it.

🏴‍☠️

Finishing Flourish

Finish a level on a long word and watch the treasure chest overflow.

🌈

Perfect Day

Clear all five levels without buying yer way through. Then brag about it.
