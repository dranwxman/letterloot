LetterLoot Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 21, 2026

LetterLoot ("we", "us", "our") is a daily word puzzle game available on the web and as a native iOS app. This policy explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how it is used. We aim to collect as little as possible and to be transparent about what we do.

1. Information We Collect

If you play as a guest: We do not collect any personal information. Your scores, settings, and game progress are stored only on your device using local storage. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

If you create an account: We collect the following:

  • Email address — used to sign you in, send password resets, and contact you about account issues.
  • Display name (nickname) — shown on the public leaderboard.
  • Optional profile photo — if you upload one, it is shown next to your name in the app.
  • Gameplay data — your scores, level times, words played, streaks, badges, and Perfect Day records. This information is used to show your progress and to populate the global leaderboard.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide the core app functionality (sign-in, save progress, sync across devices).
  • To display the global leaderboard so players can compete.
  • To respond to your support requests and account inquiries.
  • To investigate and resolve bugs or abuse reports.

We do not use your information for advertising, marketing, behavioral tracking, or profiling. We do not sell, rent, or trade your information to third parties.

3. Third-Party Service Providers

We use the following service providers to operate the app. They process data only on our behalf, under their own privacy policies:

  • Supabase — hosts the database that stores account and gameplay data.
  • Vercel — hosts the web version of the app.
  • Resend — sends transactional emails (password resets, account notifications).
  • Apple — distributes the iOS app and handles in-app purchases (if applicable).

4. Analytics, Ads, and Tracking

LetterLoot contains no analytics SDKs, no advertising networks, and no third-party tracking. We do not use cookies for tracking. We do not share data with data brokers. We do not link your data to other apps or services.

5. Children's Privacy

LetterLoot is a general-audience word puzzle and is rated 4+. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account without parental consent, please contact us at hello@letterloot.net and we will delete the account promptly.

6. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from inside the app:

  • Open the Menu (in-game)
  • Tap Account → Delete Account
  • Confirm by typing DELETE

This removes your account, your gameplay records, and your leaderboard entries. Word reports you have submitted are anonymized (your name is replaced with "[deleted]") so we can continue to evaluate dictionary issues without keeping your identity.

If for any reason you cannot delete in-app, email hello@letterloot.net and we will process the deletion within 30 days.

7. Data Retention

We keep account and gameplay data for as long as your account exists. When you delete your account, the data is removed (or anonymized, in the case of word reports) immediately. Backups containing the data are rotated out within 30 days.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete the personal information we hold about you. To exercise any of these rights, email hello@letterloot.net from the email address associated with your account.

9. Security

We use HTTPS for all data transmission. Passwords are hashed by our authentication provider (Supabase) and are never stored or visible to us. We follow standard practices to protect your information, but no online service can guarantee perfect security.

10. Changes to This Policy

If we make material changes to this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app or by email. Continued use of LetterLoot after a change indicates acceptance of the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions, requests, or concerns? Email hello@letterloot.net.
