Common Topics

  • Trouble signing in or resetting your password — email us with the address on your account and we'll help.
  • A word wasn't accepted that you think should be — use the in-app "Report Word" option, or email us with the word.
  • Account deletion — you can delete your account anytime from Menu → Account → Delete Account. If you have trouble, email us.
  • Bug reports — please include your device model and iOS version if possible.
  • Feature requests — we love these. Send them our way.

How to Play

LetterLoot is a daily word puzzle where every letter has a point value. Build words from the tiles on the board, stack bonus tiles for multipliers, and hunt for the hidden Loot Letter each day. Complete all 5 levels for a Perfect Day.
